OCR language data (tessdata)

eng.traineddata is the English language model loaded by Tesseract OCR at run
time. It is a data file, not program code. It comes from the official
Tesseract OCR project's trained-data repositories:

  https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata
  https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata_fast
  https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata_best
